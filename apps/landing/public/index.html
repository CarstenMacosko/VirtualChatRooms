VirtualChatRooms Early preview · invite only

Family calls where everyone's in the same room.

Make a cartoon avatar of yourself, walk around a living room together, and talk like you're actually there. Voices come from where people stand, so you can wander over for a quiet chat.

How it works

Got a link from family? Just open it. Kids can join as guests, no account needed.

How it works

🧑‍🎨

Your own avatar

Start from a selfie or pick everything yourself. With your camera on, your avatar copies your smiles and surprised faces. Off, it still talks and blinks.

🛋️

A room to share

Walk around a 3D living room together, sit close, wave, dance. Soon you'll be able to scan your own home with your phone and invite everyone over.

🎧

Voices where people stand

People nearby sound close, and people across the room sound far away. Walk over to someone to talk, just like at a real get-together.

📹

Faces, too

A FaceTime-style strip shows everyone's camera beside the room. Pin someone to see them big.

Built for families

  • Kids join safely. Accounts are 13+. Younger kids join with a guest link from a parent, and nothing about them is stored.
  • Your home stays private. A room only opens while its owner, or someone they trust, is hosting.
  • Hosts stay in charge. Mute, turn off cameras, lock the room or ask someone to leave.
  • Calls ring like calls. Ring the whole family at once, even when their app is closed.

VirtualChatRooms is in early development. Made for families who live far apart.
