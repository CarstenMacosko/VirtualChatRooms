# ADR 0004: VRM 1.0 avatars

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Plan reference:** D4 in [PLAN.md § 2](../../PLAN.md#2-key-technical-decisions)

## Decision

Avatars use the open VRM 1.0 humanoid format.

## Why

Fixed humanoid skeleton and expression set, no vendor lock-in, works with IK for VR.

## Alternatives considered

Vendor avatar SDKs (lock-in, several have shut down); custom rigs.
